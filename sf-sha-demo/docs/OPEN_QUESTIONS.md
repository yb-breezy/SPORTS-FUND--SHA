# Questions for the Sports Fund and SHA

These must be answered by the institutions before any official system is built. They are grouped by the discussion areas proposed in §12 of the concept note. Numbers (Q1, Q2 …) are referenced from the requirements matrix.

## Beneficiary scope
- **Q1.** Who is eligible in each beneficiary group, and who confirms eligibility?
- **Q2.** What is the minimum official set of beneficiary data fields? Is a national ID or SHA number needed, and is that lawful and necessary?
- **Q3.** May gender be recorded for inclusion reporting (§7), and with what categories?
- **Q4.** Which programmes and counties would an initial pilot cover?

## Institutional coordination
- **Q5.** Who would the real users be, and what should each role be allowed to see and do?
- **Q6.** Would sports federations or county stakeholders use the system directly, or only send information to Sports Fund staff?
- **Q7.** Should beneficiaries have any self-service access to see their own registration and follow-up status?

## SHA engagement
- **Q8.** Would SHA permit any coverage-verification exchange? Under what formal agreement?
- **Q9.** What official technical documentation, authentication and testing environment would SHA provide?
- **Q10.** Which data fields may be sent to SHA and returned, and what are SHA's real status categories?
- **Q11.** How would errors, mismatches and reconciliation be handled?
- **Q12.** What acceptance testing and approvals are required before go-live?
- **Q13.** Is there an approved list of accredited facilities that referral guidance could use?

## Programme and finance
- **Q14.** What are the real case categories, priorities and expected resolution times?
- **Q15.** Who may create, update and close cases?
- **Q16.** How are programme budgets and expenditures structured today, who approves entries, and which existing financial system is the source of truth?
- **Q17.** Which reports does management need, how often, and what baseline and targets would be set?

## Governance and data protection
- **Q18.** Who is the data controller and processor? Is a data-protection impact assessment required? How are consent, privacy notices and data-subject rights handled under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019?
- **Q19.** How long must records and audit logs be kept, and who may review them?
- **Q20.** What joint coordination and oversight body would govern the platform?

## Next steps and sustainability
- **Q21.** Where would a real system be hosted, who secures and maintains it, and what availability is expected?
- **Q22.** Who owns and funds the platform after a pilot, and what is the process to move from concept discussion to a formal feasibility assessment?
